*Example note for the Research Notes section.*

Elimination removes variables from a polynomial system to obtain conditions on the remaining variables.

## A simple example

Consider

$$
y-x^2=0, \qquad y-1=0.
$$

Eliminating $y$ gives $x^2-1=0$. The original system has the two solutions $(1,1)$ and $(-1,1)$.

[[notes/resultants|Resultants]] provide an algebraic tool for elimination. If we care about solutions over $\mathbb{R}$, we also need to consider [[notes/real-feasibility|real feasibility]].

## A question to keep in mind

When does a condition obtained by elimination suffice to recover a solution of the original system?
